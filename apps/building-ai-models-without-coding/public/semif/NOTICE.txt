Local Jev uses the direct allowed-token scoring method from SemIf.
Source: https://github.com/TheoLeeCJ/SemIf
Pinned source: 1f2dea3e25379f9dfc98cb83c324f00ab5deda37
Runtime: SemIf's vendored wllama 3.6.1. See SEMIF-LICENSE.txt and WLLAMA-LICENSE.txt.
Changes: two fixed display labels (Slop/Clear), direct readout only, single CPU thread, request identifiers, validation, and explicit worker cancellation. No text is submitted to a remote inference service.

Models are downloaded directly from immutable Hugging Face revisions:
MiniCPM5-2B-Q4_K_M.gguf, openbmb/MiniCPM5-2B-GGUF, revision 2079a22f3beaa4e306449978533478fe0522f4b3, Apache-2.0.
Qwen3-0.6B-Q8_0.gguf, Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B-GGUF, revision 23749fefcc72300e3a2ad315e1317431b06b590a, Apache-2.0.
Licenses checked in each pinned Hugging Face model card on 2026-09-22.
Model weights are not included in this repository.

Scores are normalized over the two allowed label tokens. They are not calibrated probabilities and cannot establish who wrote a text or whether its claims are true.
